# Finishing your setup

**In short:** Confirm your store details, set your Partner app password and choose the hours when you can pack and hand over orders.

## Complete the setup

1. **Check your store details.** Confirm the name, contact details, business category and collection location. If the collection address is wrong in Shopify, correct it under **Settings > Locations**. See [[Your-Locations]].
2. **Add your Shipping & Returns URL.** Use your customer-facing policy page so customers can find your return information.
3. **Set your password.** Follow the password step. If it asks for a temporary password, use the one in your setup email.
4. **Set your operating hours.** Choose when staff can accept, pack and hand over orders. You can copy a day's hours across the week. If you skip this step, finish it in the Partner app before going live.

After setup, sign in to the [STREET Partner app](https://retailer.street.london) and check the correct store/location and hours. Then follow [[Choosing-Your-Products]] and the [[Going-Live-Checklist]].

## If setup is interrupted

Your store-details draft is saved in the same browser when browser storage is available. Keep setup in the same tab while you set your password and hours.

If the hours page asks you to log in again, open the Partner app to finish setting your hours. For password or account problems, contact [[Get-Help|support]].
