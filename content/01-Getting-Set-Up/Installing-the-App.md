# Installing the STREET app

**In short:** Install STREET in Shopify, approve billing, then follow the link to complete your retailer profile.

## Before you start

Have access to your Shopify admin and permission to install apps and approve charges. STREET onboarding expects a UK store serving the supported London area, GBP currency, Shopify Payments and standard Shopify Checkout. Contact [[Get-Help|support]] if your setup differs.

## Install and connect

1. Open the STREET installation link supplied by the onboarding team and install the app in Shopify.
2. Review and approve the requested access. [[How-STREET-Connects-To-Your-Store]] explains what STREET adds.
3. Approve STREET billing in Shopify. If the approval page does not open, use **Approve billing on Shopify** in the STREET app.
4. After returning to the app, follow **Next: complete your retailer profile**. Continue with [[Finishing-Your-Setup]].

If the **STREET account** card says **No account connected**, use **Connect** and follow the account-connection flow. Contact support if your existing account does not connect.

## Before going live

Installation does not complete onboarding. Review your catalogue and location before going Online: products can already be enabled for STREET. Use the [[Going-Live-Checklist]] to check the remaining steps.
