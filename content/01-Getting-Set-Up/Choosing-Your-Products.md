# Choosing your products

**In short:** Review which products are On Street before going Online, including switching off anything you do not want to offer.

## Review products in Shopify

1. In Shopify admin, open the **STREET** app, then **Catalog**.
2. If a location selector is shown, choose the branch you want to review.
3. Use each product's **On Street** checkbox to enable or disable it for that location.
4. Check the resulting selection, variants and available stock before making the location live.

New products can start enabled. An empty setup form does not mean that all products are off. If a selected product is still missing from STREET, follow [[Getting-Visible-On-STREET]].

## Bulk changes and location settings

Select products, then use **Enable on Street** or **Disable on Street** for a bulk change. The table header selects the current page; **Select all** extends the selection across results.

> Watch out: bulk actions change the shared product setting, even when a branch is selected. They can affect other branches, while existing location-specific settings still take precedence. Use individual product controls for a branch-specific change.

## Other catalogue controls

You can also open **Catalog** in the Partner app and use the **On Street / Off Street** switch. Check which location's catalogue you are managing.

To limit the quantity shown for a variant, see stock allocation in [[STREET-and-Your-Shopify-POS]]. Product descriptions, prices and inventory are maintained in Shopify.
