# Setting up your counter tablet

**In short:** Keep the Partner app open on a charged counter device, and check that order alerts work before you go Online.

## Install the Partner app

Open [retailer.street.london](https://retailer.street.london) and log in with your STREET account.

- **iPad:** in Safari, open **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android tablet:** in Chrome, open the browser menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
- **Computer:** in Chrome, use the install control in the address bar when available.

Open the installed app from its icon. An older device may work, but browser and alert support vary. [[Get-Help|Support]] can help check a device that does not pass the checks below.

## Check alerts before your shift

1. Keep the device charged and the app open. After reopening it, tap **Orders**.
2. Open **Store** and check **NOTIFICATIONS**. Look for **Order alerts are on**.
3. If alerts are off, use **TURN ON**. If browser permission is blocked, use **FIX** and follow the device instructions. The orders screen may also show **Order alerts are off** with **FIX NOW**.
4. Check audible device volume and that the screen stays awake. Confirm the order chime during your first order walkthrough with the onboarding team.

If the status says **Not supported in this browser**, or stays at **registering this device**, contact support. The absence of a warning banner is not proof that alerts work. Screen wake support also varies, so check the device during the shift.

## When an order arrives

An incoming order plays a repeating chime until answered. Open it and follow [[Accepting-Orders]] within the short acceptance window.

## Staff and shared devices

Ask [[Get-Help|support]] to arrange staff accounts. Staff at the same location can see its orders. When someone handles an incoming order, the alert clears on other connected devices; accepted orders remain accessible, so agree who is packing each one.

A phone can be a useful backup when you step away. Check its alerts too.
