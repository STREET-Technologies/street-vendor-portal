# Accepting orders

**In short:** Open the incoming order and choose ACCEPT or DECLINE within the short acceptance window.

## Accept an order

1. Check the requested items and variants, and that your location can prepare the order.
2. Select **ACCEPT**.
3. Open the accepted order and follow [[Packing-and-Collection]].

If the app says the order was handled on another device, check **LIVE ORDERS** and agree who is packing it.

## If you cannot fulfil it

Select **DECLINE**, choose a reason, then **CONFIRM**:

- **Too busy**
- **Missing items / Out of Stock**
- **Store Closing**

Check that the incoming order has cleared. If you choose **Store Closing** on a single-location account, the app also pauses the location. Check **OFFLINE** and follow [[Your-Locations]] when ready to reopen.

If you have already accepted and discover a missing item, use the item controls in [[Packing-and-Collection]] instead of leaving the order waiting.

> Watch out: an order not accepted in time is marked missed and cancelled. It is not passed to another shop. If you cannot take new orders, pause the location rather than relying on the acceptance window to expire.
