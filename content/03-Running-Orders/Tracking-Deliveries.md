# Tracking deliveries

**In short:** Open the order in the Partner app to check collection and delivery progress; use Chat if STREET needs to investigate.

## Follow a live order

Open **Orders > LIVE ORDERS** and select the order. Completed orders are under **ORDER HISTORY**, where you can use **Search history**.

The status distinguishes finding a courier, the rider heading to your shop, collection and delivery. [[Order-Status-Flow]] explains the labels. A packed order may still be waiting for a courier; Shopify's fulfilled status is not proof that the customer has received it.

## If progress looks wrong

Check your connection and use **Retry** or **Refresh** if the order fails to load. If the status still looks wrong or the rider has not arrived, open **Chat** and include the order number, location and status shown. STREET support handles courier and delivery problems; see [[Get-Help]].

## If the courier returns the order

**Returning to Store** means the courier could not complete delivery and is bringing the order back. **Returned to Store** means it has come back. Open the order for the reason, where available, and contact support if you need help.

This differs from a customer returning goods after delivery. Check the existing refund before issuing one yourself; see [[Returns-and-Refunds]].
