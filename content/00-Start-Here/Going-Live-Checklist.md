# Going-live checklist

**In short:** Check the location, catalogue and order device before opening your shop on STREET.

## Setup and products

- [ ] STREET is installed in Shopify and billing approval is complete. See [[Installing-the-App]].
- [ ] Store details, password, policy URL and operating hours are checked. See [[Finishing-Your-Setup]].
- [ ] The collection address is correct for each location you plan to open. See [[Your-Locations]].
- [ ] On Street products and variants are reviewed, including anything to switch off. Stock and allocation match the intended location. See [[Choosing-Your-Products]].
- [ ] The STREET shipping card confirms the delivery method is configured. See [[Getting-Visible-On-STREET]].

## Staff and the order device

- [ ] The installed Partner app opens on the correct account/location.
- [ ] The device is charged, stays awake and shows **Order alerts are on**. Check volume and confirm the chime during an order walkthrough. See [[Setting-Up-Your-Counter-Tablet]].
- [ ] Staff know who will accept and pack orders, how to use the bag count and READY, and what to do if an item is unavailable. See [[Packing-and-Collection]].
- [ ] Staff can find [[Get-Help|Chat and support details]] and know where to look up [[Returns-and-Refunds|returns]].

## Open and check

Use the availability controls in [[Your-Locations]] when staff are ready. Check the correct branch is open and follow [[Getting-Visible-On-STREET]] if the shop or products are missing.

Arrange a first-order walkthrough with the onboarding team to check alerts, packing and tracking together. Agree the order and payment arrangements before placing it.
